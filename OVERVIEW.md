# Survos OTIO

Standalone PHP editorial-document and timing library planned around an OpenTimelineIO subset. The broader story workflow retrieves relevant platform evidence before compiling a sourced story into OTIO and a browser playback projection.

Current state: Composer metadata and design documents only. First implementation: upstream fixture preservation and cut/gap/trim timing. Symfony UX, Remotion playback, federated retrieval and archival reconstruction remain planned. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).
